## ⚡ ملخص SAD Ch4: Requirement Gathering

- **Requirement** = بيان لقدرة/خاصية/جودة لازم النظام يكون عنده · **unambiguous, testable, necessary** (IEEE).
- **أهميتها:** أساس Planning · Risk Management · Trade-off · Acceptance Testing · Change Control.
- **Complete** (كل المرافق) و**Consistent** (بدون تعارض) · عمليًا مستحيل 100%.
- **معايير الجيد:** Necessary · Feasible · Correct · Concise · Unambiguous · Complete · Consistent · Verifiable · Traceable · Allocated · Design independent · Nonredundant · **shall** · unique ID · no escape clauses.
- **فشل 1995:** Incomplete requirements **13.1%** · Lack of user involvement **12.4%**.
- **نجاح 1995:** User involvement **15.9%** · **2015:** Executive management support **20%**.
- **Functional:** خدمات وسلوك (behavioral/operational) · مثال MHC-PMS · **Imprecision** مثال 'search'.
- **Non-functional:** خصائص وقيود · **Sommerville (3):** Product · Organisational · External · **Robertson (8):** Look-and-feel · Usability · Performance · Operational · Maintainability · Cultural · Legal · Security.
- **Security:** Access · Privacy · Integrity · Immunity · هجمات: Disclosure · Modification · Denial · Repudiation.
- **Fit criteria:** Speed · Size · Ease of use (training time) · Reliability (MTTF) · Robustness · Portability.
- **Constraints:** Solution · Deadlines · Budget · Current system.
